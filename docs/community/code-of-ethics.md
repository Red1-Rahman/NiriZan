## 1. Preamble & Core Philosophy

Computing technology and software solutions deeply influence individuals, organizations, and society as a whole. The **NiriZan** repository is built on the fundamental premise that software development is not merely a technical endeavor, but a social and ethical responsibility.

This **Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct** establishes the core moral principles, professional standards, and community guidelines governing all development, maintenance, contributions, and interactions within the NiriZan project. Every maintainer, contributor, reviewer, and community member is expected to uphold these principles in good faith, ensuring that our collective work consistently supports the **public good**, respects human rights, and fosters a safe, inclusive, and collaborative environment.

---

## 2. General Ethical Principles

### 2.1 Contribute to Society and Human Well-Being
* **Public Good First:** All contributions to NiriZan must consider the broader impacts on society, individual well-being, and digital safety.
* **Human Rights & Autonomy:** Software tools and systems developed in this repository should respect individual autonomy, fundamental human rights, and diversity.
* **Environmental Responsibility:** Contributors are encouraged to write efficient, optimized code that minimizes unnecessary resource consumption and computational waste.

### 2.2 Avoid Harm and Mitigate Unintended Consequences
* **Defining Harm:** Harm includes, but is not limited to, physical or mental distress, unjustified data exposure, security vulnerabilities, property or system damage, and societal disenfranchisement.
* **Proactive Mitigation:** Maintainers and contributors must carefully assess potential risks, unintended side-effects, and systemic edge cases. When harm or vulnerabilities are identified, prompt action must be taken to correct, patch, or mitigate the issue.
* **Responsible Deployment:** Features or capabilities that present predictable or unavoidable ethical risks or malicious exploitation vectors should be reconsidered or omitted.

### 2.3 Honesty, Trustworthiness, and Transparency
* **Integrity in Communication:** Be truthful and transparent regarding system capabilities, performance benchmarks, limitations, known issues, and technical debt.
* **Conflict of Interest:** Disclose any real or perceived conflicts of interest that could compromise independent judgment or project integrity.
* **Accurate Attribution:** Do not falsify claims, fabricate data, misrepresent project roadmaps, or obscure third-party contributions.

### 2.4 Fairness, Inclusivity, and Non-Discrimination
* **Equal Opportunity:** The NiriZan project welcomes contributors from all backgrounds regardless of age, race, ethnicity, nationality, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, neurodivergence, or level of experience.
* **Anti-Harassment Policy:** Harassment, bullying, personal attacks, exclusionary jokes, hate speech, and abuses of power will not be tolerated under any circumstances.
* **Accessibility:** Strive to design software, user interfaces, documentation, and APIs to be as accessible and inclusive as possible.

### 2.5 Respect Intellectual Property and Open Source
* **Credit Creators:** Respect copyrights, licenses, patents, and trademarks. Always provide appropriate attribution for code, algorithms, datasets, or documentation derived from open-source works.
* **License Compliance:** Adhere strictly to the open-source license under which NiriZan is distributed, as well as the licenses of any upstream dependencies.
* **No Plagiarism:** Claiming ownership of work created by others or distributing non-compliant proprietary code within the repository is strictly prohibited.

### 2.6 Respect Privacy and Data Protection
* **Data Minimization:** Only collect, process, or store personal data that is strictly necessary for legitimate system functionality.
* **Consent & Transparency:** Clear disclosure and user consent must precede any data aggregation, telemetry, or user tracking.
* **Anonymization & Security:** Safeguard data against unauthorized access, re-identification, or accidental disclosure.

## 3. Professional & Technical Responsibilities

### 3.1 Pursuit of High Technical Quality
* Maintain high standards of code quality, test coverage, code readability, and architectural soundness.
* Respect project maintainability and provide clear, comprehensive documentation for all major features and modules.

### 3.2 Professional Review and Constructive Feedback
* Peer review is an essential mechanism for ensuring software correctness, security, and quality.
* Pull request reviews and issue discussions must be constructive, objective, respectful, and focused on technical merit rather than personal attributes.

### 3.3 Security, Vulnerability Management, and Bug Bounty
* **Secure Design:** Systems must be designed and implemented with robust security practices from the outset.
* **Responsible Vulnerability Disclosure:** Security researchers and community members are requested to report vulnerabilities privately to [redwanrahman2002@outlook.com](mailto:redwanrahman2002@outlook.com) prior to public disclosure.
* **Timely Remediation:** Maintainers commit to addressing confirmed security flaws with urgent patch releases and transparent risk advisories.

### 3.4 Work Within Areas of Competence
* Contributors and maintainers should evaluate tasks thoughtfully and seek advice or collaboration when addressing domains outside their expertise (e.g., cryptography, legal compliance, complex data privacy).

## 4. Leadership & Community Governance

### 4.1 Maintainer Commitments
As leaders of the NiriZan project, maintainers commit to:
1. Prioritizing the public interest and community safety over short-term technical shortcuts.
2. Creating a welcoming environment that supports psychological safety and professional growth for all contributors.
3. Applying repository policies, issue triage, and code reviews fairly and consistently without favoritism.
4. Managing software evolution, feature deprecations, and breaking changes thoughtfully to minimize disruption for users.

### 4.2 Conflict Resolution
When technical or interpersonal disputes arise:
* Discussions should remain grounded in facts, project goals, and ethical principles.
* Maintainers will mediate conflicts with neutrality, empathy, and transparency.

## 5. Reporting Violations & Enforcement Policy

### 5.1 How to Report an Incident
If you experience or witness unethical conduct, harassment, security breaches, or violations of this Code:
* Contact the project maintainer, **Redwan Rahman**, via email at **[redwanrahman2002@outlook.com](mailto:redwanrahman2002@outlook.com)**.
* Include as much relevant context as possible: links to issues/PRs, timestamps, description of the incident, and any supporting documentation or screenshots.
* All reports will be handled with strict confidentiality to protect the privacy and safety of the reporter.

### 5.2 Enforcement Process
Upon receiving a report, the maintainer will:
1. **Acknowledge:** Confirm receipt of the report within 48 hours.
2. **Investigate:** Review the reported behavior, surrounding context, and impacted parties objectively.
3. **Determine Action:** Take appropriate corrective measure based on the severity of the violation:
   * **Private Correction:** A private written warning providing clarity on the nature of the violation and expected behavioral change.
   * **Public Warning:** A formal public reprimand or closure of relevant thread/issue/PR.
   * **Temporary Suspension:** Temporary ban from contributing or interacting with the repository.
   * **Permanent Ban:** Permanent removal from the repository organization, commit access, and community spaces.
