# UN Sustainable Development Goals Mapping

## Scope

The United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are a global policy framework comprising 17 goals and 169 targets under the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development.

NiriZan is mapped only to SDGs with a direct and defensible thematic connection to its purpose as open-source infrastructure for continuous evaluation of AI systems. A check indicates project-level alignment, not achievement, measurable impact, endorsement, or formal SDG compliance.

## Goal Mapping

| UN Sustainable Development Goal | NiriZan |
|---|:---:|
| SDG 9 — Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure | ✅ |
| SDG 12 — Responsible Consumption and Production | ✅ |
| SDG 16 — Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions | ✅ |
| All other SDGs | ⬜ |

## Justification

- **SDG 9 — Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure** — NiriZan provides reusable open-source infrastructure for evaluating RAG systems, AI agents, LLM applications, and other AI workflows. Its instrumentation, evaluation, regression detection, trust analysis, and deployment-gating capabilities support engineering infrastructure for reliable AI development and experimentation.

- **SDG 12 — Responsible Consumption and Production** — NiriZan promotes more responsible production of AI software by detecting quality regressions, statistical changes, and evaluator reliability problems before degraded systems are accepted into deployment workflows. Its continuous-evaluation approach encourages teams to measure system quality rather than rely solely on assumptions about model or application behavior.

- **SDG 16 — Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions** — NiriZan emphasizes transparent and reproducible evaluation through versioned runs, stored evaluation evidence, statistical regression analysis, explicit gate verdicts, and judge-reliability analysis. These mechanisms align thematically with the SDG's broader emphasis on accountability, transparency, and effective decision-making.

## Applicability Note

This is a project-level thematic mapping, not a claim that NiriZan independently achieves or measurably advances any UN SDG target. The mapping intentionally excludes goals for which the project has no direct mechanism of action or demonstrated evidence of impact.
